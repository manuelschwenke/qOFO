The OFO controllers themselves. The TSO controller dispatches AVR setpoints, OLTCs and shunts and issues Q setpoints downward; the DSO controller tracks those setpoints with local actuators and reports capability and tracking error upward. Controllers never see the plant: they act only on cached sensitivities and their own measurements.

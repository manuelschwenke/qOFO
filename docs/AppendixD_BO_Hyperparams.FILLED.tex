What is here is the material that supports it: the coordinates and the weight blocks each generates, the measured relations from which the admissible region is computed, the ranges swept, and the tables the campaign is to fill.

\section{Coordinates and the Weights They Generate}\label{ch:app_bo:space}
\phantomsection\label{ch:ofo:tuning:bo:problem}% legacy label (former Ch 9 §9.3.1)
\phantomsection\label{ch:ofo:tuning:bo:space}% legacy label

The decision space is the six coordinates of \cref{tab:param:weights:coords}, not the weights themselves.
Each coordinate is a quantity that can be stated in engineering units, each maps to a whole coherent block of weights through the generating rule of \cref{eq:param:weights:recurrence}, and the redundant common-scaling direction of \cref{ch:param:weights:why} is absent from the space by construction, because the rule fixes the gauge once.

The three nominal-gain coordinates $\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}}$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{TS}}$ and $\lambda_{\mathrm{DS}}$ are settled against the isolated-loop screen of \cref{eq:param:weights:budget}; the three remaining coordinates $\tau$, $\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}}^{(\gls{DSO})}$ and $r_{v}$ are trade-offs for which the plant supplies no value, and are settled by the search of \cref{ch:app_bo:scenarios}.
The \gls{AVR} move budget is a seventh quantity, held rather than chosen: it pins the gauge against which every other weight is expressed, so moving it would shift the numeraire rather than the design.

Two conventions fix the remaining freedom.
The allocation coordinate $\tau$ is gauge-fixed to a geometric mean of one over the two actuator classes it splits, so that $\tau$ and $1/\tau$ exchange the classes rather than rescaling both.
The tracking weights follow from stated output tolerances through $g = \sigma^{-2}$, so the quantity defended in writing is a tolerance in per unit or Mvar rather than a weight of arbitrary magnitude, and $r_{v}$ is the explicit exchange rate between interface tracking and local voltage compliance.

\section{The Admissible Region}\label{ch:app_wsel:region}
%************************************************

The campaign tests two proposed relations that would reduce the nominal gain screen to a formula in the two \gls{TS} coordinates.
Both are properties of the network, its area partition and the boundary model, and must be re-measured if any of those changes.

\paragraph{The tap penalty is affine in the commit threshold.}
The curvature rule generates the \gls{OLTC} move penalty from the commit threshold as
\begin{equation}
	g_{w}^{\mathrm{OLTC}}(\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}}) = a_{1}\,\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}} + a_{0},
	\qquad a_{1} = \num{318846}, \quad a_{0} = \num{-999},
	\label{eq:app_wsel:gwoltc}
\end{equation}
fitted over the threshold values of \cref{tab:app_wsel:region}, maximum residual \num{0.33}, i.e.\ \qty{0.005}{\percent} of the mean penalty.

\paragraph{The nominal upper eigenvalue is a floor plus a gain.}
The discrete columns cannot be scaled away by any continuous weight and so impose a floor, which falls as the taps are priced more dearly; the continuous gain then adds to it linearly,
\begin{equation}
	\lambda_{\max}(\MAT{B}) = \frac{c_{0}}{g_{w}^{\mathrm{OLTC}}(\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}})} + c_{1}\,\lambda_{\mathrm{TS}},
	\qquad c_{0} = \num{4104}, \quad c_{1} = \num{1.5846},
	\label{eq:app_wsel:rho}
\end{equation}
fitted over the $(\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}},\lambda_{\mathrm{TS}})$ grid of \cref{tab:app_wsel:ranges}, maximum residual \num{0.0142}, over its stated validity range $\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}} \geq \qty{0.015}{\perunit}$.

\paragraph{Allowance for the operating point.}
Both relations are evaluated on sensitivities cached at a single operating point.
Across five operating points spanning the profile year, $\lambda_{\max}(\MAT{B})$ at fixed coordinates varies over \numrange{1.344}{1.399}, the worst lying \qty{2.1}{\percent} above the mean. The bank-to-bank shift of the measured statistic is larger and subsumes it: between the design bank and the confirmation bank it is \qtyrange{2.9}{3.5}{\percent} over six measured candidate pairs, and resampling twelve-window banks from the pooled windows gives \qty{3.1}{\percent}. The \emph{worst} observed shift is carried, not the mean: a candidate selected at \num{1.4554} under a \qty{3}{\percent} allowance was measured at \num{1.5031} out of sample and rejected. Hence $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{op}} = \num{0.035}$.
That allowance gives the design target and admissible region
\begin{align}
	\bar{\lambda}&=\frac{1.5}{1+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{op}}},
	&\varepsilon_{\mathrm{op}}&=\num{0.035},
	\label{eq:param:weights:target}\\
	\lambda_{\mathrm{TS}}&\leq\frac{1}{c_{1}}
	\left(\bar{\lambda}-\frac{c_{0}}{a_{1}\Delta\Spannung_{\mathrm{eng}}+a_{0}}\right),
	&&\label{eq:param:weights:region}
\end{align}
obtained by combining the nominal screen with \cref{eq:app_wsel:gwoltc,eq:app_wsel:rho}.
The relation is a hypothesis until the campaign has measured its coefficients and residuals; its boundary is tabulated in \cref{tab:app_wsel:region}.

The nominal screen is evaluated per isolated controller and must not be read as a test of the physical interconnection.
For every candidate, the separate coupled diagnostic of \cref{eq:param:weights:blocks} therefore reports both $\rho(\MAT{A}_{\mathrm{cl}})$, which tests asymptotic decay of the linearised synchronous update, and $\lVert\MAT{A}_{\mathrm{cl}}\rVert_{2}$, which tests one-step contraction in the displayed coordinates.
Neither quantity certifies the nonlinear, constrained, multi-rate implementation; the full-plant cases remain decisive.

\paragraph{Identifiability probe.}
Before the search, each coordinate is probed one at a time over a fixed span about its analytic value with all others held.
A coordinate whose probe is flat is not identifiable \emph{on this design bank} and is excluded, so that it cannot absorb budget in the search.
The probe is read against all three costs and not against $f_{V}$ alone: a coordinate can be nearly invisible to the supervisory objective and decisive for the criterion it is actually responsible for.
A flat probe is a statement about the excitation the bank supplies, not about the coordinate, and a bank of \qty{90}{\minute} windows resolves what an actuator does within an event but not what it accumulates over a day.
A coordinate excluded on this evidence keeps the value the design rule gives it and is reported as undetermined by the search.

\paragraph{Design bank.}
\phantomsection\label{ch:app_wsel:bank}%
Five \qty{90}{\minute} windows on one network variant, each admitted because it excites one coordinate of \cref{tab:param:weights:coords} rather than for coverage: a quiescent summer night, as the control against buying stress performance with chatter at rest; a generator outage at spring noon, where \gls{DER} reactive capability is full and the continuous loop gain is therefore identifiable; a winter-evening load ramp and its summer mirror, which exhaust the continuous reactive range and hand authority to the tap changers; and a sign-reversing spring window, the only construction in the bank under which tap reversals occur at all and a hunting criterion carries information.
Each window opens with \qty{15}{\minute} of stabilisation before its first event, so no coordinate is graded on a transient of the initialisation.

\paragraph{Confirmation bank.}
Three further windows of the same construction, calendar-disjoint from the design bank by parity: design windows fall in odd calendar weeks, confirmation windows in even ones, so the sets share no week rather than merely no timestamp.
Step~4 of the procedure re-evaluates the chosen point and the analytic baseline on this bank, and only improvements that survive are carried forward.

\paragraph{Capability strata.}
Below \qty{10}{\percent} of rated power the applicable capability diagram \cite{VDARN4120.2018} gives exactly zero reactive range, which holds for \qty{18.6}{\percent} of the profile year.
In those windows $\tau$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DS}}$ and $r_{v}$ have nothing to allocate.
Both banks sample all three capability strata in proportion, and costs are reported per stratum as well as in aggregate.

% WITHDRAWN with the BO method: the composite scalar cost J_s and its CVaR
% aggregation across scenarios. Both are replaced by a three-cost filter with
% no weighted sum, for the reason stated below. The superseded definitions are
% in the archived file.

Three costs are recorded, all minimised, all normalised and dimensionless:
$f_{V}$, the \gls{TS} voltage cost, combining \gls{RMS} deviation, worst bus and band excess; $f_{Q}$, the interface reactive-power tracking error; and $f_{V}^{(\gls{DSO})}$, the \gls{DS} voltage cost.

They are \emph{not} collapsed into a weighted scalar.
All three are controlled outputs of the architecture, so all three belong in the comparison, and a single weighted objective would let the search spend one to buy another without that being visible in the reported number.
The search therefore reports the non-dominated set on the three together, and the design point is a declared choice on a small frontier rather than an optimum.
\todo{Quantify the cost of collapsing them, on the campaign data: report the $f_{V}^{(\gls{DSO})}$ loss incurred by the point that maximises the $f_{Q}$ gain when $f_{V}^{(\gls{DSO})}$ is omitted from the comparison. The chapter asserts this trade exists; this table is what makes it a measurement.}

Two constraints filter the frontier before the choice is made.
Voltage-corridor compliance and settling are checked per window in the usual way (\cref{ch:setup:metrics}).
Switching wear is measured separately, in \cref{ch:app_wsel:results}.

\paragraph{Failure handling.}
\phantomsection\label{ch:app_bo:robustness}% legacy label
\phantomsection\label{ch:ofo:tuning:bo:robustness}% legacy label
The trial wrapper never raises.
Any exception during simulation is captured in a failure-reason string and a sentinel cost is returned; the only exceptions deliberately propagated are programmer-bug validation errors that signal a malformed parameter dictionary.
A candidate that fails is therefore excluded from the frontier with a finite record rather than terminating the campaign.
